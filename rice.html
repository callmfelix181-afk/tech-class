RICE IS A STAPLE FOOD IN MANY COUNTRIES.

it is a cereal grain that is widely consumed staple food for a large part of the world's population

it is the agricultural comodity with the third higest worldwide production

There are many different varieties of rice, including long grain, medium-grain, and short-grain rice

Rice can be cooked in various ways, such as boiling, steaming, or frying
